Designed for Every Clinical Role

Streamlined workspaces tailored specifically to hospital roles with real-time transactional synchronization.

Hospital Administration

Complete hospital operational dashboard. Audit bed occupancy levels, manage billing logs, and inspect HIPAA-compliant activity audit trails stored securely.

Clinician Workspace

Manage OPD queues, record digital SOAP consultation notes, issue e-prescriptions, and submit lab orders instantly. Query patient medical history with a RAG AI Assistant.

Ward & Vitals Tracker

Real-time inpatient monitoring. Record temperature, heart rate, BP, and blood oxygen levels, with automated warning triggers for patients in critical state.

Pharmacy Center

Dispense pending doctor prescriptions, monitor medication stock alerts, and toggle prescription states with automated financial tracking integrations.

Lab Diagnostics

Process specimen collections, track test requests, and publish numerical lab outputs. Instantly notify doctors when results are ready.

Patient Care Portal

Enable patients to book doctor appointments, view active prescription files, retrieve certified lab test results, and clear pending invoices online.
